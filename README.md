# Academic projects

Selected coursework from my B.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering
at Ben Gurion University of the Negev. The writeups are in Hebrew.

    Analog Circuits/                      final course assignment and my worked solution
    Integrated Circuit & Intro to VLSI/   CMOS inverter lab and a Verilog-A SAR ADC

The analog circuits assignment is solved end to end in a 19 page writeup.
The VLSI labs were done in Cadence Virtuoso, a CMOS inverter drawn and
simulated at schematic level, and an 8 bit successive approximation ADC
modelled in Verilog-A from the counter and register blocks upward.

My main project is the FPGA image pipeline in
[FPGA_project](https://github.com/naftalimason/FPGA_project).
